GOOD PLACES TO START MILESTONE SEQUENCE How the resident milestones build on each other The resident portal and the MuleSoft integration layer come first. Each later milestone starts only once the ones before it are delivered. Timing will be set with the City's project plans. 1 MILESTONE 1 TOP PRIORITY Resident Portal • Self-service portal for residents, built on Experience Cloud • MuleSoft is built here and powers the portal, point of sale and all future portal enhancements 1.5 MILESTONE 1.5 PREREQUISITE Point of Sale • Delivered right after the portal and tied into it 2 MILESTONE 2 SECONDARY Mobile App + Agentforce for Staff • Gives residents mobile access to the same portal • Agentforce for Service helps customer service staff answer and resolve requests 3 MILESTONE 3 FINAL MILESTONE Resident Service Agent • An AI agent that answers resident questions and handles service requests GATE 1Portal live GATE 2Portal and point of sale live; decision on later phases GATE 3Portal, point of sale, app and staff agent complete Foundation · MuleSoft integration layerBuilt in Milestone 1 and reused by point of sale, the mobile app and both agents. Connects Vadim, ActiveNet, Cartegraph and Workday. In place · Service Cloud CRM + KnowledgeEvery milestone builds on the City's existing CRM and knowledge base. Separate track · IT Service ManagementIn evaluation. Not part of the resident milestones or their gates, and follows its own decision timeline. Shared foundations: build once, use everywhere • One knowledge base serves front-counter staff, the portal, the app, the resident agent and IT self-service, so residents and staff get the same answer everywhere. • Reusable APIs (starting with tax and billing) feed the staff console first, then the portal, app and agent. Channels stay protected when back-office systems change. • Employee data from Workday can drive onboarding and offboarding tasks in IT service management, and power an employee help agent. • Payments taken on the platform still post to the finance system, so payments and integration should be planned together. • Better tools for customer service staff also reduce IT requests, since front-line teams touch the most systems. Decisions ahead for the City 1 Platform role: a corporate service platform, or a departmental tool? 2 Integration ownership: an in-house team, a partner, or a mix, sized to the City's capacity. 3 Sequencing with the finance system: build connections now, or align with the replacement timeline? 4 Resident identity: one login across portal, app, recreation and payments? 5 AI guardrails: what data agents can use, in line with MFIPPA, AODA and cybersecurity priorities. 6 Funding approach: phased investments aligned to annual budget cycles and procurement rules.
